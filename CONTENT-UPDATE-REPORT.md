## 1. Content Quality Overview

All 28 published articles on SaaS Launch Lab were audited for search intent alignment, technical depth, factual accuracy, E-E-A-T signals, structural readability, and AI search extractability (Google AI Overviews, Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude).

### Core Interventions
1. **Thin Content Remediation:** Expanded 5 thin articles that previously fell below 900 words, elevating each piece to 1,380 – 1,540 words with production code, architectural diagrams, and structured decision frameworks.
2. **Neutralization of Fabricated & First-Person Claims:** Replaced unverified personal claims ("After 15+ years in SaaS...", "From my experience...") with objective, data-backed architectural principles.
3. **Restoration of Broken Structure:**
   - Restored missing `## Mistake #8: Outsourcing Content Creation Without Editorial Oversight and E-E-A-T Standards` in `9-common-saas-seo-mistakes.md`.
   - Restored missing `### 6. In-House Brand & Creative Teams` and deleted stray internal SEO prompt notes in `who-develops-brand-identity-for-saas-companies.md`.
   - Repaired mangled markdown in Case Studies 1, 2, and 3 in `mvp-development-cost.md`.
   - Completed truncated sentences in `saas-database-design.md`, `what-is-white-label-saas.md`, and `why-most-saas-mvps-fail.md`.
4. **100% FAQ Coverage:** Added structured, direct-answer FAQ sections to the 2 articles that lacked them (`9-common-saas-seo-mistakes` and `mvp-development-cost`), ensuring all 28 articles qualify for FAQPage schema markup.
5. **Authoritative External Documentation Citations:** Integrated primary-source citations to official standards bodies and industry documentation (PostgreSQL, W3C, IETF RFCs, OWASP, Nielsen Norman Group, Stripe, ACM).

## 3. Google AI Overviews & LLM Citation Optimization

To maximize visibility across Google AI Overviews, Google Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, articles were updated with:
- **Direct Answer Opening Paragraphs:** Every article immediately defines the core concept or directly answers the primary user search query in the first 2–3 sentences.
- **Structured Data Tables:** Replaced prose lists with scannable Markdown comparison tables (e.g., Agency vs. Freelancer, SQL vs. NoSQL, Cloud Hosting Tiers, Cost Allocations).
- **Executable Code & SQL Schemas:** Added production-ready PostgreSQL Row-Level Security scripts and index statements that AI models index as authoritative technical references.
- **Consistent Terminology:** Standardized terms across all articles (e.g., "Multi-Tenant Architecture", "Row-Level Security (RLS)", "Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)", "Webhook Idempotency").
